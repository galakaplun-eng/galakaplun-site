About

I believe the most personal stories are also the most universal.

Hi! I'm Gala, a video editor and storyteller, and a proud graduate of Sapir College's Audio & Visual Arts program with a degree in film studies.

I’m passionate about storytelling. Every project feels like stepping into a new world, and I thrive on uncovering and shaping visual stories that resonate.

Staying ahead in a fast-evolving industry, I embrace the latest trends and technologies to create fresh, impactful content. Whether it's crafting an emotional narrative or delivering high-energy visuals, I aim to bring your ideas to life in unforgettable ways.

Always seeking new challenges and diverse projects, I’d love to connect and create something extraordinary together.
